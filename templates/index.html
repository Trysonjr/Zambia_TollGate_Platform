PROBLEM STATEMENT 1 Automated Self-Service Tollgate
Automated Tollgate Operations & Barrier Control

Manual cash toll collection creates heavy bottleneck delays, long queues, and audit friction on Zambian highways (e.g. Great East Road and Kafue corridor).

Implemented Working Capabilities:
  • • ANPR Camera Simulation: Optical plate recognition with confidence metrics.
  • • Simulated RFID/E-Tag Scan: DSRC windshield transponder interrogation.
  • • Zambian Fee Calculation: Automatic pricing (Light K20, Medium K50, Heavy K100).
  • • Dual Payment Path: Instant account deduction or simulated Mobile Money.
  • • Visual Boom Barrier Simulation: 90-degree arm animation (CLOSED → OPEN → CLOSED).

PROBLEM STATEMENT 2 Mobile Toll Account & Central Management
Centralized Motorist Accounts & Administrative Governance

Absence of unified motorist self-service accounts impedes seamless highway mobility and prevents transport authorities from monitoring revenue and traffic flow centrally.

Implemented Working Capabilities:
  • • Secure Motorist Authentication: Werkzeug scrypt hashing & session control.
  • • Vehicle Registration: Linking plates to RFID/E-Tags & vehicle types.
  • • Simulated Wallet Funding: Instant top-up via Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, Zamtel.
  • • Admin Executive Intelligence: KPI cards, plaza revenue charts, traffic mix.
  • • Traceable System Audit Log: Immutable logging of all actions and logins.

University Presentation Demonstration Script (21-Step Workflow)
Examiner Guide

Follow this exact sequence to demonstrate the integrated capabilities to the academic assessment panel:

Simulation Flow
  1. Open Tollgate Simulation.
  2. Select Preset: ABC 1234 (Light Vehicle, K150).
  3. Click Vehicle Arrives (ANPR scan).
  4. Click Verify & Deduct K20.00.
  5. Watch barrier animate: CLOSED → OPEN → CLOSED.
Motorist Verification
  1. Open Transactions to see the newly generated crossing.
  2. Log in as Motorist (motorist@test.zm).
  3. Verify updated balance: K150.00 → K130.00.
  4. Test Fund Toll Account (+K100 via Airtel Money).
  5. Register a new vehicle with RFID tag.
Admin & Future Scope
  1. Log in as Admin (admin@tollgate.gov.zm).
  2. Review Total Revenue & Plaza Traffic charts.
  3. Inspect the security Audit Log trail.
  4. Navigate to System Modules (70%) to present the full proposed national architecture.
